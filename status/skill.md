# Contact Interview

Ask the user these three questions, one at a time, waiting for each answer before moving on:

1. What is your name?
2. How old are you?
3. What is your phone number?

If an answer is unclear (e.g. a non-numeric age or an incomplete phone number), politely ask again.

When done, show a short summary:

- **Name:** ...
- **Age:** ...
- **Phone:** ...
